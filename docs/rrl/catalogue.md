## How these relate to the chosen thesis topic

**Chosen topic:** _An Empirical Complexity Analysis of Candidate-Narrowing Strategies for Incremental LLM-Driven Graph Construction_ — comparing brute-force, embedding-threshold, ANN-index, and bucket-capped strategies for inserting new nodes into an LLM-built graph, measured as cost and placement accuracy as the graph scales from n=50 to n=2000+.

- **#8 (EraRAG)** is the direct anchor — it has the only real theorem in the entire set, and its gap (never plotting cost vs. existing graph size) _is_ the thesis's core empirical question.
- **#3, #4, #7 (Funk et al. → iText2KG → DIAL-KG)** form the lineage the four candidate-narrowing strategies are drawn from: #3 gives strategy justification (brute-force doesn't scale) and the KRIS pruned-search alternative; #4 gives strategy 2 verbatim (its exact threshold); #6 (SAC-KG) and #7 give the expand-or-stop/entity-matching mechanisms that motivate why matching quality is the load-bearing component worth measuring.
- **#5 (Co-STORM)** supplies the only existing head-to-head strategy comparison found anywhere — and confirms _no one_ has done this for more than two strategies or at varying graph sizes, which is precisely the empirical gap the thesis fills.
- **#9 (ACE)** and **#12 (Wang & Li)** were central to the _earlier_, now-abandoned education-framed version of the topic. They remain relevant as a possible gold-standard-data source (ACE's Metacademy/DSA graphs) and as evidence that the general problem-space is real, but they're no longer load-bearing for the current pure-CS framing per Luke's explicit pivot.
- **#1, #2 (the two surveys)** are context-setting rather than directly cited in the final design — they established that the _build_ phase is solved/reviewed, which is what pushed the thesis toward the _insertion_ phase as the actual open ground.
- **#10, #11** are peripheral: useful for methodology borrowing (a real connectivity metric; the KT-loop framing) but neither targets insertion-cost-vs-graph-size, so neither is a direct comparator for the final design.

Two open items from the original transcript that the catalogue surfaces but doesn't resolve: the gold-standard placement-accuracy dataset (reuse ACE's graphs vs. hand-build one) was still undecided at handoff, and you'd just been offered a choice between pseudocode for the four strategies or locking that dataset decision first — worth picking back up if this thread is continuing that work rather than just archiving it.
